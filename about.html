Learn with Shen

Making Additional Mathematics easier to understand, one concept at a time.

THE IDEA

Learn the concept. Understand the method. Solve the problem.

Learn with Shen is an educational platform designed for Malaysian SPM Additional Mathematics students.

The goal is to provide structured explanations, examples and learning resources that help students understand mathematics instead of simply memorising formulas.

More lessons, examples and videos will be added progressively.
